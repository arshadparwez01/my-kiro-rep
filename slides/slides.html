The Problem

Modern stacks are
noisy & fragmented.

A typical enterprise runs hundreds of cron jobs, custom schedulers, ETL pipelines, cloud functions, and SAP batch jobs — all loosely connected. When one breaks, everyone finds out from the business.

!
Silos everywhere.  Cron on Linux, Task Scheduler on Windows, Airflow for data, native SAP jobs — nothing talks to each other.
?
Zero end-to-end visibility.  When a downstream job fails, no one can quickly trace the upstream cause.
$
Missed SLAs hurt the business.  Late month-end close, delayed invoicing, broken reports — real revenue impact.
⚠
Audit & compliance gaps.  Manual interventions and shell scripts leave little trace for auditors.

Spotlight Use-Case

Why SAP needs
real orchestration.

SAP is the digital backbone for finance, supply chain, HR and operations. But SAP rarely lives alone — data flows in and out of dozens of upstream and downstream systems every single hour.

A typical SAP day looks like this:
Source files land from suppliers → loaded into SAP S/4HANA → BW/4HANA process chains crunch the data → reports refresh in SAC / BusinessObjects → outputs sent to banks, regulators & partners.

Miss one step — month-end close slips.
