Spotlight Use-Case

Why SAP needs
real orchestration.

SAP is the digital backbone for finance, supply chain, HR and operations. But SAP rarely lives alone — data flows in and out of dozens of upstream and downstream systems every single hour.

A typical SAP day looks like this:
Source files land from suppliers → loaded into SAP S/4HANA → BW/4HANA process chains crunch the data → reports refresh in SAC / BusinessObjects → outputs sent to banks, regulators & partners.

Miss one step — month-end close slips.
